Glowstone Grove Privacy Policy

Liquidpixel Studio · Last updated 2 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how Glowstone Grove: Gem Match (the "Game"), an Android game published on Google Play by Liquidpixel Studio ("we", "us"), handles your information.

Summary

Glowstone Grove does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. The Game works fully offline and does not connect to the internet.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The Game does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal details, and it has no accounts or sign-in.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (such as levels, stars, coins, your Grove and settings) locally on your device so you can carry on where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions

The Game only uses the vibration permission, to give haptic feedback during play. The Game does not request internet access.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

Glowstone Grove contains no ads, no analytics or tracking tools and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The "Living Sky" feature works out the moon phase from your device's date and time only.

Purchases

Glowstone Grove is a paid game bought through Google Play and has no in-app purchases. Payments are handled entirely by Google, and we do not receive your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles purchase information.

Children

The Game is suitable for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, email liquidpixelstudio1987@gmail.com.
